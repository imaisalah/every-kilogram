# Orbit validation

Generated by `node tools/validate-orbits.mjs --write`. Constants are typed into the script from the NASA NSSDC planetary fact sheets, not read from the game.

**What this shows:** the game's period and shadow time match an independent two-body calculation for every orbit that uses the standard formulas (12 orbits; worst period difference 0.27%, worst shadow difference 0.01 percentage points). The small differences come from the game using mean planet radii. A separate three-case GMAT cross-check is recorded below; neither check validates the full flight simulator.

## Real missions with a published period

The published periods below are from NASA and USGS fact pages as we remember them. **Re-check each against its page before quoting it.**

| Mission | Published (min) | Computed (min) | Diff % |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landsat 9, 705 km sun-synchronous | 99.0 | 98.9 | -0.1 |
| International Space Station, about 410 km | 92.7 | 92.8 | 0.1 |
| Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter, 255 x 320 km (mean height used) | 112.0 | 113.1 | 1.0 |

Europa orbits (the flyby tour and low Europa orbit) are not checked here: the tour is a sequence of flybys, not one orbit.

"Shadow" is the share of each orbit spent in the planet's shadow when the Sun lies in the orbit plane, the worst case. Real orbits that are tilted toward the Sun spend less time in shadow.
